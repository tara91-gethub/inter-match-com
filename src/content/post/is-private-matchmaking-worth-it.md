Private matchmaking can be worth it if you want a serious relationship but prefer to keep your photos and personal details off dating apps. It can also help if you already know what you are looking for but would like some help meeting the right person.

## Who Is Private Matchmaking For?

Some people come to us because they value privacy. Others have demanding careers or busy lives and do not want to spend their free time looking through profiles. They are not looking for more matches. They want to meet someone kind who is also ready for a serious relationship.

There is nothing wrong with dating casually. But private matchmaking may not be right for you if you are still deciding what you want or expect an introduction right away. It is a better fit when you know you want a relationship and are ready to take the time to get to know someone.

## What Can a Dating Profile Tell You?

A profile can show you where someone lives, what they enjoy and what they say they want. It cannot tell you how they listen, how patient they are or whether they are ready to make time for a relationship.

Someone who could be a good fit might live in another city or just across the border. A profile can show you how far away they live. A conversation can help us understand whether both people are willing to travel and make time to see each other.

## We Speak to People Before Making an Introduction

Dating apps bring together people from different backgrounds who may have very different ideas about relationships. Two people can both say they want something serious, but one may be ready to build a life with someone while the other is still exploring. It can also be hard to know whether the details on a profile are accurate.

That is why we speak to both people before making an introduction. We ask what they are looking for, listen to how they talk about relationships and check the details we reasonably can. Then we decide whether we think they would be a good fit for each other.

## What Are You Paying For?

You are paying for the time and work that go into finding someone we think is worth introducing. We get to know you, speak with potential matches and make each introduction with care. We would rather take more time than send you profiles just to show progress.

You are paying for a service, not a promise that you will find a partner. You can read more about [how we make introductions](/matchmaking-process).

## One Question to Ask Before You Pay

Ask any matchmaker: **"What happens if you do not find anyone you think is right for me?"**

Our answer is simple. We would rather tell you we have not found someone suitable than introduce you to someone just to give you a match.

Finding love can be one of the hardest things in life. You never know when you will meet someone who wants the same kind of life you do. Sometimes it starts with one introduction, and that one introduction can change your life.
